About OET Practice

OET Practice is an online preparation platform for healthcare professionals preparing for the Occupational English Test (OET).

What We Offer

Reading & Listening Practice

We provide online Reading and Listening practice tests to help candidates become familiar with OET-style tasks, manage their time, and practise with greater confidence.

Writing & Speaking Feedback

Our platform also offers optional Writing and Speaking feedback services for candidates who need focused support with profession-specific communication tasks.

Who It's For

OET Practice is designed for doctors, nurses, and other healthcare professionals preparing for registration, work, study, or professional opportunities in English-speaking settings.

OET assesses Listening, Reading, Writing, and Speaking, with Writing and Speaking reflecting profession-specific healthcare scenarios.

How we support each skill

  • ✓ Reading — online practice tests
  • ✓ Listening — online practice tests
  • ✓ Writing — optional expert feedback
  • ✓ Speaking — optional expert feedback
