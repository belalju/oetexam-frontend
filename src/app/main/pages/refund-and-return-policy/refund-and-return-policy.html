This policy applies to paid Reading and Listening practice-test access purchased from OET Practice.

1. Refunds for Unused Purchases

2. Reallocate Access to Another Email Address

If no practice test has been attempted under your purchased package, you may request that the unused access be reassigned to another email address.

Please provide:

  • Your original purchase email address.
  • Your order reference or proof of payment.
  • The new email address to which access should be assigned.

Once reassignment is completed, access under the original email address will be removed. Reassignment does not create an additional account entitlement or duplicate access.

We will confirm the applicable access period before completing the reassignment.

3. Delay Access for Up to Six Months

If you have purchased a package but have not attempted any practice tests, you may request a delayed start for your access.

You may choose a new start date up to six months from your original purchase date. The purchased access period will begin on the agreed new start date.

During the approved delay, access to the paid practice tests will be paused. Please contact us before attempting a test so that we can arrange and confirm the change.

A delayed start is not a cash refund or an extension beyond the agreed package duration.

4. Requests After a Test Has Been Attempted

Once any practice test within the purchased package has been attempted, the unused-purchase reassignment and delayed-start options described above are no longer available.

This restriction does not affect any rights you may have under applicable consumer-protection law.

5. Payment Errors and Access Problems

If you believe you have been charged incorrectly, charged more than once, or have not received the access you purchased, email us with your order details.

We will investigate and provide an appropriate resolution. Where applicable law requires a refund or another remedy, we will honour that obligation.

6. Writing and Speaking Evaluation Services

This policy covers practice-test access only. Fees, cancellation conditions, and any refund arrangements for Writing or Speaking evaluation will be provided separately before payment.
